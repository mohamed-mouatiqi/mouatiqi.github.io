Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the PDF Converter app ("the App") handles your information.

1. Files you convert

All conversions (images to PDF, Word to PDF, page reordering and compression) happen locally on your device. Your images, documents and generated PDFs are never uploaded to our servers. We do not have servers that receive your files.

2. Permissions

Images are selected through the Android system Photo Picker, and documents through the system file picker. The App only accesses the files you explicitly choose. Generated PDFs are stored in the App's private storage, or in a location you choose when you save or share them.

3. Advertising

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address and general usage data to show ads and measure their performance. This data is processed by Google according to its own policy: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data in ads.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads from your device's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

4. Data we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. We do not require an account or sign-in.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
